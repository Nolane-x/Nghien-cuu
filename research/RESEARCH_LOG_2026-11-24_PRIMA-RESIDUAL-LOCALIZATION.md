**Date:** 24 November 2026  
**Topic:** source/output localization of 7-port versus 9-port transient errors  
**Branch:** research/prima-residual-localization-2026-11-24

## Research question

For the four matched morphology holdouts, how does the nine-port / seven-port shared-output transient NRMSE ratio vary by input source, output location, pulse width and passive parameter scenario?

## Hypothesis

The relative penalty observed for NMO_163775 at basis order 168 is not uniform across source and output space. Because seven-port reference errors are already very small, large ratios may coexist with very small absolute NRMSE values. Both must be reported.
