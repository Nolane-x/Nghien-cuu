# Research log — Segev passive AC source-alignment sensitivity — 2026-11-21

## Question

Does the source reference selected by NEURON's passive impedance solver materially change input/transfer impedance when switching from the legacy 0.125 µm base-mesh segment centre to a nearby SWC-aligned segment centre, with one identical refined mesh?

## Rationale

The Segev multi-morphology passive AC matrix was reduced to sub-0.2% transfer-complex maximum errors after fixing an Import3D soma/axon attachment omission. The remaining error is frequency- and source/probe-dependent. A separate DC source-placement control shows that source alignment varies by cell and source site, but it cannot establish the AC sensitivity.

## Sources and method

- Morphologies: NeuroMorpho NMO_68174 / cell1125 and NMO_68175 / cell1204, unchanged CNG SWCs with CC BY 4.0 attribution in the data directory.
- Reference simulator: pinned NEURON 9.0.2.
- Source selection, Import3D section mapping and local nseg refinement reuse the existing Segev DC source-placement implementation.
- On the same final mesh, compare two `Impedance.loc(x)` positions: the legacy base-mesh centre and the SWC-aligned centre.
- Query the same five exact SWC probe coordinates at eight frequencies from 0.1 to 3,000 Hz.
- Keep passive parameters fixed: Ri=0.7 Ω·m, Rm=1.0 Ω·m², Cm=0.01 F/m² and Eleak=−65 mV.
- Record complex impedance deltas, magnitude deltas and signed phase deltas for both input and transfer impedance.
- Exclude pre_aligned and unresolved_nseg_cap rows from sensitivity summaries. Preserve them in raw output with explicit status labels.

NEURON API reference: https://www.neuronsimulator.org/en/9.0.0/progref/analysis/programmatic/impedance.html

## Limits

This is a passive within-NEURON numerical sensitivity experiment. It is not biological validation and cannot establish human cortical parameter accuracy. The two morphologies have incomplete axons and should not be treated as a representative population. Keep FCI-derived reduced active parameters, NMO_290988 passive assumptions, Segev cell morphologies and the unavailable original Eyal 2018 archive as separate named configurations.
