## Intended use

This is an independent **control-condition** morphology holdout for passive linear-RC PRIMA shift/order validation. It is from a different archive and experimental context than the NMO_162186 epilepsy specimen. The source has a three-point soma and includes a partial axon, basal dendrites and apical dendrites.

The initial source inventory found 869 records with type counts: soma 3, axon 42, basal dendrite 816, apical dendrite 8. The CI preflight independently recomputes and pins the original downloaded SHA-256, exact record count, parent topology, Import3D mapping, mesh cap and seven unique PRIMA ports. The final immutable checksum and electrical compartment count are recorded in the generated \`NMO_03524_JacobsControl_holdout/preflight.json\`.

## Limitations

- A single morphology is not a population sample.
- “Control” is the published archive metadata; this does not establish absence of every neurological condition beyond the study's stated classification.
- The dendritic reconstruction is rated moderate and the axon incomplete.
- The experiment validates passive linear-RC numerical reduction only. It does not validate active ion channels, nonlinear synapses, action potentials, plasticity or biological function.
- The morphology is used as a numerical holdout, not to estimate or tune biological conductances.
